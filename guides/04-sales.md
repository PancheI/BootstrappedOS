# Head of Sales

**Your first paying customers in 30 days.** It finds the people who already have your problem, checks the signal, the fit and your history with them, and suggests an angle. You write the messages and send them.

## Set it up (20 minutes, once)
1. Type `/first-customers` and add one line about what you sell, for example `/first-customers bookkeeping for small agencies`.

   **You should see** Day 0: your one buyer, three real quotes of the problem in their words (each with a link), and a price. It waits for your yes before saving them to `knowledge/decisions.md`.
2. Say yes, or tell it what to change.

## Every day (about an hour, with coffee)
1. Type `/first-customers`.

   **You should see** today's step. In weeks 1-3: today's list of people, each with where they asked, a short quote, why they fit, your history with them, and a suggested angle. In week 4: four numbers and the one drop to fix.
2. Or ask for buyer signals directly: "Find me 3 to 5 posts on X, LinkedIn or Reddit from the last week that ask a question my product answers. Link each one. Don't write replies."

   **You should see** a one-page report in `knowledge/reports/`: 3 to 5 real people, each with a link, a date and a quote.
3. Write your replies yourself. Want a starting point? Ask for a draft for one person; it comes back marked as a draft.

## What a good result looks like
```
1. Shop owner, Reddit r/smallbusiness, 2 days ago
   "orders dropped since the autumn sale, no idea why"
   Fits: online shop, says the pain in money. History: none.
   Angle: ask how long a product page takes to load on their phone.
   Options to mention: PageSpeed Insights, their theme's image setting, your audit (last, with "I built this").
```
You write: "open one product page on your phone and count. If the photo takes more than 3 seconds, that's likely where the orders went. What does it look like on yours?" Their words, free help, one easy question, no link.

## One memory
Every person, reply and skip goes in `knowledge/customers.md`, one dated line each. Support and Content see who you talked to, and the follow-ups never get lost.

## Avoid
- **Letting AI write and send your messages.** Replies that read like a bot lose the deal. AI finds the people; you write.
- **Pitching in the first message.** Help first. Your product goes last, with "I'm the founder, so I'm biased".
- **Skipping follow-ups.** Most deals close on a follow-up, not the first message.
