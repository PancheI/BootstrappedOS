# Head of Growth: launch

**Your product gets found.** A findable site, a listing kit, the directories that fit, pitches and a launch-week plan, all drafted. You sign in, submit and post.

## Set it up (30 minutes, once)
1. Add your site to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, and submit your sitemap. The agent tells you each step; you sign in.
2. Type `/launch-week` with your product and the launch date, for example `/launch-week https://yoursite.com 3 Nov`.

   **You should see** in order: the fixes that make your site findable, a listing kit filled from real facts, 8-12 directories (each marked dofollow, dofollow with badge, paid or nofollow, with a reason), 3 pitches, the launch-day posts, and a day-by-day plan with a tracking link per listing.

## Launch week (30 minutes a day)
1. Each morning, open the plan in `knowledge/trackers/launch.md` and do today's items.
2. Submit each listing yourself, with its own tracking link. Mark it done in the tracker.
3. On launch day, post asking for feedback, never for votes.

## What a good result looks like
```
Directory         Link type          Why                          Status
Indie Hackers     dofollow           your buyers read it          submitted 3 Nov
dev.to post       dofollow           a how-to with your numbers   draft ready
Medium post       nofollow           reach, not links             launch day
<paid list>       paid               skip: a link, not customers  -
```

## One memory
Every listing, its link type and its status goes in `knowledge/trackers/`. Next month the Monday review shows which sources sent real visitors.

## Avoid
- **Expecting directories to send buyers.** They give links, not customers. Reach comes from posts and people.
- **Paying for listings.** Use the free ones first.
- **Letting an agent submit or post for you.** It drafts; you sign in and click.

The directory list, with how to submit each and what its link is worth, comes from the Bootstrapped core ([CONNECT.md](../CONNECT.md)). Without it, the agent checks each site live.
