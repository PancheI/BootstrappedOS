# Head of Content

**Posts and a newsletter made from real things that happened this week.** Plus lead magnets: one free, useful thing that gets your buyer's reply or email. You edit and publish.

## Set it up (15 minutes, once)
1. Paste 3 of your best past posts and ask: "Save my voice to `knowledge/how-to/voice.md`."

   **You should see** a short voice file: how you open, how long your lines are, words you use and words you never use.

## Every Monday (20 minutes)
1. Type `/content-week`.

   **You should see** questions about this week's wins, mistakes and customer questions, then 3 post drafts and 1 newsletter draft.
2. Something worked? Type `/post-from-a-win` with what happened and the real number.

   **You should see** a post under 150 words (a hook, short lines, the number, one lesson, one question), then a 3-line X version.
3. Edit, then post it yourself.

## What a good result looks like
```
I ran a free workshop for new managers this week. 19 came.

They all asked the same thing: how do I say no to my old teammates?
My answer: ...
My mistake: ...

What would you have told them?

Source of "19": the sign-up sheet, 14 Oct.
```

## Once a month: a lead magnet
Type `/lead-magnet`. **You should see** 3-5 real buyer questions with where each came from, then one proposed format and the promise. It waits for your yes, then drafts the magnet, one proof post, a DM and 2 follow-ups.

## One memory
It reads `knowledge/how-to/voice.md` and `knowledge/numbers.md`, so posts sound like you and use only numbers you logged. Each number's source is listed under the draft.

## Avoid
- **Invented stories or numbers.** Only what happened, with its source.
- **Links in the post.** Put them in the first comment.
- **"Excited to announce" with no lesson.** Say what happened and what you learned.
