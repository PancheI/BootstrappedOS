# Head of Grants

**Grants and AI credits, no equity.** This week: the AI, cloud and tool credits you qualify for, checked on the official page. Over time: grants that fit, with the application drafted. You apply.

It can't apply for you or promise you'll get anything. Grants take weeks to months; credits can close within days.

## Set it up (20 minutes, once)
1. Make sure your `CLAUDE.md` says your country, whether you have a company, your stage and your tools. `/grants` asks once for anything missing.
2. Type `/grants`.

   **You should see** a ranked list, closing-soon first. Each row has the value as stated, who qualifies, the deadline, what you give back, the official link and today's date. Then a draft for the top one.

## Every month (20 minutes)
1. Type `/grants` again.

   **You should see** the tracker updated: new programmes added, closed ones moved to "closed".
2. Fill the `[NEED]` gaps in the draft, then apply yourself on the official page.

## What a good result looks like
```
Program            Type     Value (as stated)   Deadline   Status          Link   Checked
<AI lab credits>   credits  as on the page      rolling    ready for you   ✓      22 Oct
<cloud startup>    credits  as on the page      30 Nov     drafting        ✓      22 Oct
<national grant>   grant    as on the page      15 Jan     fits            ✓      22 Oct
```
No amount or deadline from memory. Missing facts are marked `[NEED]`.

## One memory
The tracker lives in `knowledge/trackers/grants-and-credits.md`. Your reusable answers live in `knowledge/how-to/grants-answers.md`, so each application is faster than the last.

## Avoid
- **Trusting a blog list.** Only the official page counts, checked today.
- **Waiting on a credit offer.** Act the day you hear about it; some close within days.
- **Missing the fine print.** Check whether a programme takes equity before you sign up.

Which programmes are open now comes from the Bootstrapped core ([CONNECT.md](../CONNECT.md)). Without it, the agent searches official pages itself.
