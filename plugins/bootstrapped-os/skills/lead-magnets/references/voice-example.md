# Build `knowledge/how-to/voice.md` from your best posts

A lead magnet and its posts sell on trust. Trust comes from sounding like the founder, not like every other AI draft. So before the first draft, build a voice file from posts that actually worked, with their numbers. Every hire that writes reads it first.

## How to build it (about 30 minutes)
1. **Collect posts.** Export or copy the founder's last 12 months of posts (LinkedIn, X, newsletter), each with reactions, comments and the date. Fewer than 10? Use the 3 the founder is proudest of and add to it later.
2. **Sort by result.** Take the top 5 and the bottom 5 by reactions (or replies, or sign-ups, if known).
3. **Tag each post** with simple traits: has a real number? announces something that happened? thanks people? a pitch? a forecast? language? length?
4. **Compare medians** for each trait, with vs without. Only write a rule where the gap is large and the sample isn't tiny.
5. **Copy the shapes** of the top posts: first line, line length, how they end.
6. **Write the file** (template below). Under one page.
7. **Ask the founder:** "Does this sound like you?" Save only after yes. Date it; redo it every quarter.

## How the author does it (his voice file, 137 posts)
Built from his own posts over 12 months (137 of his own posts; median reactions). *(Source: his post archive and voice analysis.)*

| Trait | With | Without |
|---|---|---|
| Announces a real result ("live", "launched", sign-ups) | 90 | 15 |
| Thanks people | 41 | 13 |
| Written in his home language, to his local network | 103 | 14 |
| A forecast or trend ("by 2028…") | 10 | 17 |
| A pitch ("apply", "spots left", "pilot") | 8 | 16 |

Sweet spot: 500 to 1,300 characters. Under 500 did poorly.

**How he writes** (from the winners):
- First line is news or a verdict, 2-8 words. "40 days ago I launched…"
- A founder reporting from inside the build, not an expert explaining a market.
- Exact numbers as a funnel, never rounded: visitors -> sign-ups -> started -> first sales.
- One idea per line, blank line between.
- Humble, thanks people, soft close or none. No "Comment X below".

**What flopped:** pilot-programme announcements, forecasts, long explainers, "Thrilled to…" event recaps.

**What it means for lead magnets:** his magnets travelled best inside posts that reported something real (a launch, sign-ups, a finding), in his home language, with a thank-you. Pitch posts did about half as well as posts without a pitch. So the magnet rides on news; it is never the headline itself.

Your traits and numbers will differ. A founder with an international audience may find English wins. Measure yours; don't copy his.
