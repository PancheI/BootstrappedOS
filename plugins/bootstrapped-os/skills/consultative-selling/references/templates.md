# Consultative selling: the messages

Lowercase and short is fine; it reads like a person on a phone. Change the words every time.

## Thank first
- Connection note to someone who liked the post: `thanks for liking the post; would love to connect`
- Thank-you DM to engagers: `Hi [name], thanks for supporting my post about [the post in one plain line].`
- Next day, same chat, to people who replied: `doing a [call / session] on [topic] on [day]. want a seat?`

## Open with something lived, or one question
- First DM (trigger + value + ask): `saw you liked the [post about topic]. [their gap, in their words] is what i spent the last year solving. documented the whole setup. want me to send it?`
- One lived fact: `[provider] held my [amount] for [N] days. no human on the other end.`
- One question about their setup: `which [tool] are you on today? [option a] or [option b]?`
- To a fellow builder: `ye [thing] always breaks. how you doing it in [their product]?`

## When they ask about your product: answer in one line, then ask back
| They ask | You answer (one line) | You ask back |
|---|---|---|
| "What does it cost?" | `[price], [what's included].` | `what do you pay for this today, in money or hours?` |
| "Does it do X?" | `yes / not yet.` | `how are you handling X right now?` |
| "Does it support [teams / languages / volume]?" | `yes, [limit].` | `how many [people / clients] go through it in a week?` |
| "How is it different from [rival]?" | `[the one real difference].` | `what made you look beyond [rival]?` |
| "Can I try it?" | `yes: [link].` | `what would it need to do in the first week for you to keep it?` |

Questions that tell you if the pain is real:
- `when did this last cost you time or money?`
- `what have you tried so far?`
- `what happens if you leave it as it is?`
- `who else decides on this?`

## Give options, yours last
`honestly, [tool A] does this well, [tool B] if you need [thing]. i built [yours] for [who it fits], so i'm biased.`

## Deliver what you promised (rotate the wording)
- `as promised: [link]. mostly written from scar tissue.`
- `[name], link: [url]. holler if anything sparks a question.`

## The ask, once the pain is real
- `want to try it on [their case] this week? [price] for the first month, and if it doesn't [outcome], i refund it.`
- `paid pilot: [scope], [price], done when [success criteria]. start [date]?`
- `15 minutes on [day]? i'll bring [the thing they need].`

## Debrief (in customers.md)
```
[date] [name], [role]: "[what they said, their words]"
Problem: real? often? costly? (y/n each) | Uses today: [tool / nothing]
Next step: [what] by [date] | Owner: [founder]
```
