# The positioning card (one page, `knowledge/positioning.md`)

```
# Positioning: <product>          <date>

For:        <one buyer, one moment>          (from the wedge)
Today they: <top alternative> - <what it costs them>
            <2nd> - <cost>    Doing nothing - <cost>    AI chat - <what it can't do>
Only we:    <the thing no alternative can do>     (evidence: <link or source, date>)
Outcome:    <money or time, one number, buyer's words>
Promise:    <one sentence a buyer would repeat>

The roast
- Why pay instead of <top alternative>? <one line, evidence>
- What they get back:                  <ROI from their own numbers>
- First objection:                     "<in their words>" -> <answer>

Said vs pitched: <n> of <N> buyers named <reason> in their own words; we lead with it.
Rejected:        <promises considered and why, one line each>
Ceiling for price: <from the ROI line>
```

## Illustrative example: before and after

| | Before (no positioning step) | After |
|---|---|---|
| For | freelancers who bill larger companies | the same, on the day an invoice passes its due date and the client goes silent |
| Today they | (not asked) | chase by email for weeks; a free AI chat writes a polite reminder in seconds; many absorb the loss |
| Only we | "a set of dated reminder emails" (a chat does this) | money the buyer is owed but doesn't know to claim (for example a statutory late-payment sum, verified for their country on an official source), calculated and put on the dated messages |
| Outcome | "get your invoice paid" | "claim what the late payer now owes you, in money" |
| Roast | not run; a monthly fee for templates loses to free | one recovered sum can cover the price many times over (arithmetic on the buyer's own invoice) |

The page, the price and the posts all change when the card changes. That's why it comes first.
