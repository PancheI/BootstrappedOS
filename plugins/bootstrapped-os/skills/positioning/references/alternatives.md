# What buyers really use today

Rank alternatives by how many linked signals mention them, not by which competitor you know best.

| Always on the list | Why | What to write |
|---|---|---|
| **Doing nothing** | The most common choice, and the one you actually compete with | What it costs: days lost, money written off, the stress line in their words |
| **A generic AI chat** (ChatGPT, Claude) | Free and instant for anything that is "write me a…" | What it can't do for this buyer: know their data, follow up for days, take an action, carry a legal or money rule it wasn't told |
| **What they already use** (email, a spreadsheet, their invoicing tool) | Zero switching cost | The step where it stops helping |
| **Paid tools** | Real prices set the anchor | Price read on their page today, and what users complain about, linked |

## The one-prompt test
Paste the product's promise into a generic AI chat as a prompt. If the reply is 80% of the value, the position is the 20% left over, or there is no position. Write down what was missing from the reply; that is your "only we".
