# Post shapes (from the author's own winners)

Eight shapes, classified from his own posts over 12 months. Fill them with real things only. One idea per line, a blank line between beats, an image attached, 500-1,300 characters. Write the source of every number under the draft.

| Shape | What it earns | His example (first line) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Milestone, "we did it" | reactions | "We did it. Today Agenta 2.0 is live." |
| 2. Launch-day numbers | reactions | his launch-day post (10 Aug): visitors -> sign-ups -> sales in 24 hours |
| 3. Builder moment, emotion first | reactions | "The greatest satisfaction is seeing the product… live." |
| 4. Founder journey | reactions | the company split and the solo restart |
| 5. Rage with receipts | comments | "EU is killing crypto." |
| 6. "What actually happens when…" explainer | comments | "What actually happens when you pay with card in a shop" |
| 7. Founder-pattern observation | reactions | "I talked to many founders… almost all follow the same path" |
| 8. Contrarian one-liner | reactions | a belief, then the fact that breaks it |

Shapes 1-4 earn reactions; 5-6 earn comments. Pick by the material, and never the same shape twice in a row.

---

## 1. Milestone
```
[2-8 words: what happened.] ("We did it." / "Finally.")

[What it means for the reader, in one line.]

[What it means in practice, as a list:]
- [concrete thing 1]
- [concrete thing 2]
- [concrete thing 3]

[Why you did it, one line. The mission, not the product.]

[Thanks, to the people who made it real.]
```
Optional before/after list when the news replaces an old way: "Until now, getting paid from abroad meant: 1. [platform] -> [wallet], conversion plus fees, days. 2. A direct wire, 6-8%, 3-5 days. 3. Opening a company abroad."

## 2. Launch-day numbers (his launch post)
```
[N] hours since launch. [one headline rate].

[One line: why that number matters to the reader.]

[visitors] real visitors from [N] countries (bots and your own visits filtered)
[N] sign-ups
[N] started [the key step]
[N] first sales

[What's next, one line. A cap or "spots left" only if true and counted.]

[Thanks.]
```
His launch on 10 Aug told the first 24 hours this way: visitors, then sign-ups, then the people who started the key step, then the first sales. Name what each line counts: opened, completed and live are three different numbers. Filter out your own and test visits first. Compare with a "typical" rate only if you can name its source.

## 3. Builder moment
```
[The feeling, in one sentence, first person.]

[What happened in the last N days, with one number.]

[One comparison, honest and sourced: "elsewhere this takes 2-3 weeks".]

[How, in one or two plain lines.]

[The goal, one line.]
```

## 4. Founder journey
Dated timeline, plain, humble. "In [month] we split… I continued solo. It took [N] months to find [the thing]. It wasn't adding features; it was changing [what]." Ends with "if you're in the same phase: it was worth it", never a victory lap.

## 5. Rage with receipts (his 12-section format, 1,200-1,600 characters)
1. Thesis: "It's shocking how much [hidden cost] we pay to [who]."
2. Lived hook, 2-3 lines with his own numbers (a receipt, a screenshot he took).
3. "That made me look into what [topic] actually costs."
4. What they advertise: "The headlines all say '[claim]'."
5. "What's actually in [source]:"
6. 5-10 dash bullets.
7. "this is from [source]." (lowercase)
8. The math: "You're not paying X. You're paying Y."
9. Why: one line.
10. The fix, one rhetorical line only.
11. "Drop your worst [topic] story." (not "Comment X")
12. PS: what you post about weekly.

Only with a real receipt or a public event. Without one, it's a newsletter piece.

## 6. "What actually happens when…"
One everyday action ("you pay with card in a shop", "$1,000 sent to another country"), then each hop as a line with who takes what. Ends on the total. This shape gets comments.

## 7. Founder-pattern observation
"I talked to [N] founders this month. Almost all [did X]." Only if he did and can count them. Then the one fix. Then the free thing that has the rest.

## 8. Contrarian one-liner
"[What everyone believes]." then "[The fact from your own operation that breaks it]." Two to four lines. No fact from experience = don't write it.

---

## Banned first lines
"Excited to announce", "I'm thrilled", "Here's the thing", "Let's dive in", "Nobody talks about…", "Unpopular opinion:", bold-Unicode headlines with a rocket emoji (his flopped), any "A founder told me…" that didn't happen, any forecast ("By 2028…").
