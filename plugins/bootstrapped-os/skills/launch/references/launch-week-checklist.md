# Launch week checklist

D = launch day on Product Hunt (or the one big platform you pick). Product Hunt days start at 00:01 Pacific (09:01 in central Europe). The agent drafts; the founder signs in, submits and posts.

| Day | Agent drafts | Founder does |
|---|---|---|
| D-7 | The GEO checklist from SKILL.md, run against the live site. Facts block. Prompt panel baseline. Tracker. | Fix what failed. Approve the facts block. |
| D-6 | Directory list from `bootstrapped-core` → `directories` (free only): free dofollow first, badge HTML for the badge ones. | Submit the free ones. Say yes or no to each badge. |
| D-5 | 3 Reddit threads already on Google page 1 for your prompts, each with a short brief: what the asker needs, the honest answer, where your product fits or doesn't. | Reply to them yourself, disclosed. 15 minutes. |
| D-4 | Pre-launch forum thread on the platform, as a question in the buyer's words (his: "Selling from a country your payment provider doesn't support? How do you get paid?"). Launch page: name, tagline, description, 4 screenshots of real pages, maker comment. | Post the thread. Build and schedule the page. |
| D-3 | Interview and roundup pitches (`listing-kit.md`). One dev.to post with your own data, canonical to your site. | Edit and send. Publish the dev.to post. |
| D-2 | Launch-day posts per channel, each one different. Badge row ready in static HTML. | Approve. Check the launch page on a phone. |
| D-1 | A short personal note for people who know the product: "we launch tomorrow, I'd love your feedback". | Send one by one. Never ask for upvotes. |
| D | Reply drafts as comments arrive. | Live at 00:01 PT. Reply to every comment within 15 minutes. Share in waves across time zones. Evening update comment. |
| D+1 | Results by UTM. Thank-you comment. | Post it. Thank helpers personally. |
| D+2 | FAQ entries from the best launch questions; add them to the page (and its schema, only if visible). | Approve. IndexNow ping. |
| D+7 | One page: visits, sign-ups, paying customers by source; new dofollow domains; prompt panel re-run. | Pick the next 3 channels. Log the decision. |

## Rules
- One big event per day. The author moved his launch from a Tuesday to a Thursday so it didn't clash with his own live session.
- Free launch. No promoted slot, no paid upvotes, no vote groups, no new accounts voting.
- Product Hunt links are nofollow: the launch is for visitors, sign-ups and the newsletters and roundups that copy launches, not DR.
- Swap the homepage badge if you place well.
