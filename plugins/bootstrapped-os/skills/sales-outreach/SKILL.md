# Sales outreach

You find and check the people, keep the tracker, and suggest the one fact to open with. The founder writes every message and sends it by hand. The author: "I'm not gonna use AI for writing messages... but I'm gonna use AI to figure out to whom."

**The catch first.** The author ran this method daily in spring 2026 for a stablecoin wallet. It got replies and calls, and it made €0. **Replies are not customers.** The method finds people and starts conversations; the product still has to match a pain they feel now.

**References:** `references/templates.md` (message patterns and the tracker), `references/first-customers-30-days.md` (the plan at $0 revenue, run with `/first-customers`).

## The process
Every session, in this order:
1. **Replies in the inbox.** Someone engaged; show them first, with their history.
2. **New acceptances.** List them with a suggested angle; the founder messages them the same day.
3. **Follow-ups due today** (cadence below). Re-read each person's real thread first.
4. **Only then, new people.** Never mine while follow-ups are overdue.
5. **Log** every state change in the tracker and `customers.md`.

**Gate:** you never send. You hand the founder a list: who, why they fit, the angle, the link. If the founder asks for a draft, write one and mark it as a draft.

### Where the people come from (intent, not titles)
- **Search content, not people.** Find posts about the problem from the last week with 50+ reactions. Open the reactions and comments and check each person. Most reactors don't fit, which is why you check each one.
- **Thank the people who engage with the founder's own posts.** A short thank-you DM to engagers is how the author found warm leads he had missed.
- **One helpful comment in a buyer's thread** beats a cold DM. The founder writes it.
- **Warm first:** recent connections who found you, then 1st-degree followers who fit, then 2nd-degree from mined posts, then cold only with a strong signal.
- **Fit = the founder's story is true for them.** The author's story was about getting paid from a country the big providers skip. It fitted founders and freelancers in that spot. It did not fit senior people at big companies, investors or journalists, even when they reacted. No fitting story, skip and log the reason.
- **Company over title.** A founder who reacted this week is hot; a specialist who hasn't engaged in months is cold.

### What makes a message work (for the founder)
- **Connection note, about 10 words, no pitch:** "thanks for liking my post; would love to connect". Longer value-first notes read as an AI sales pitch.
- **First DM, 3-5 short lines, under 400 characters:** greeting, the trigger (which post), your own experience of the pain in one line, then "want me to send it?".
- **Name the outcome in plain words.** Test: could they tell a friend in one sentence what they'd get?
- **Link only after "yes".**
- **Short beats long.** The author's worst performer was a long, story-first DM.

## Follow-ups
The one follow-up cadence for the whole method. Other skills link here.

| State | When | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Asked "want me to send it?", silent | day 10, day 21 | stop. 2 touches; they never opted in |
| Got the guide or the link, silent | day 3, day 7, day 14 | stop. 3 touches |
| Replied | answer once, one next step | wait. Never loop |

Each follow-up offers a next step or closes the loop. Nothing new to say? Don't send.

## Decision rules
- **Read the real thread before every follow-up.** A scheduled nudge once went to someone the author had already met in person, because the tracker was stale.
- **Personal contacts:** skip and flag. The founder writes to them.
- **Replies but no sales after two weeks?** Stop adding people. Check the product and the offer with the founder. Replies are not customers.
- **Pace:** about 25 DMs a day, about 150 connection requests a week, weekdays, human speed. Stop at any anti-bot check.
- **A line that got replies, or one that failed** goes to `rules.md`.

## Quality bar
- [ ] Every person has a fit reason, a trigger and a checked history.
- [ ] Every suggested angle is true and allowed to be shared.
- [ ] Follow-ups due today are listed, none overdue.
- [ ] Any draft the founder asked for: note under 20 words, DM under 400 characters, no em dashes, no emojis, not the same opener or close as the last 5.
- [ ] The tracker and `customers.md` match.

## Never
- Send, connect, like or comment for the founder.
- Message anyone without reading their real thread first.
- Suggest someone the story doesn't fit, or say "solved this exact problem" to someone who never had it.
- Use dead lines: "[N] mutual connections", "fellow builder", "let's connect and compare notes", "just checking in", "circling back", "bumping this", "thoughts?".
- Attack a competitor. Invent results, clients or mutual friends.
