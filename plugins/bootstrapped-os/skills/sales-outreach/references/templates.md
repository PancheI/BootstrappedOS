# Sales outreach patterns

**Patterns, not scripts: the founder writes each message.** These are the shapes of the lines the author sent, lowercase as he typed them. Use them to see what works; write your own words around your own story. Rotate the surface: if 5 of your last drafts share an opener or a close, write the next ones differently. Same payload, different words.

---

## Connection notes (≤20 words, ideal 10-15)
Use when you mined them from a post. The note gets the accept; the guide comes after.
```
thanks for liking my post; would love to connect
thanks for the support on [post]; would love to connect
appreciate the like; let's connect and see if we can collaborate
```
For people found any other way, a note about their work, never yours:
```
hey. [their product] caught my eye. solving a similar problem from the [your angle] side.
```
Senior people: `hey. [N] years in [field]. would love to connect.` (the only place a credential belongs.)

**What failed (do not send):**
- `[N] mutual connections. building [X] from [place]. [their thing] is exactly our lane. fellow builder. let's connect and compare notes.` His verdict: "this is SLOP. what does this even mean".
- `saw you liked [post]. wrote a free guide on [outcome]. happy to send once connected.` A batch of these read as an AI sales pitch.
- Ten notes in a row with the same skeleton (`[Name], [topic] + [their angle] is what I build. would love to connect.`). People get several; they compare.

## First DM: trigger, your pain, the ask
**Founders (4-5 short lines):**
```
hey [name], how are you?

wanted to share something with you. [your pain in 2-3 plain sentences: what didn't work, what it cost you].

finally figured out a better way. documented the whole thing.

want me to send it?
```
His version: "I'm from [country]. [the payment provider] doesn't work here. registered a company abroad just to accept payments. paid the lawyers, accountants, setup fees. just to collect money. finally figured out a better way. documented the whole thing. want me to send it?"

**Busy executives (3 lines):**
```
hey [name].

saw you liked the [post]. solved the [one specific side] of that. [N] tools. documented it.

want me to send it?
```

**Second story for a second buyer.** He ran exactly two: one for founders who couldn't get paid, one for people whose accountant charged extra for every incoming payment ("which token, which chain, what value in euros. every payment became a reconciliation job. finally made the problem disappear. documented how."). If neither story is true for the person, skip them.

**Too long (his worst performer):** a DM that re-explains the problem they already know before the ask. Cut everything they already know.

## Delivering the guide (after "yes"), rotate these
```
[name], link: [url]. holler if anything sparks a question.
as promised. [url]. mostly written from scar tissue.
[url]. the [part relevant to them] is in section 3.
open this: [url]. roast it if you find anything weird.
```

## Follow-ups
The days come from the cadence in `../SKILL.md` (section "Follow-ups").

**Sequence A: asked "want me to send it?", silent** (they never opted in, so low pressure)
- 1st (day 10): `hey [name]. still useful? can drop the link or we can just jump on a call.`
- 2nd (day 21): `hey [name]. parking this. ping me if it comes up.`
- Stop.

**Sequence B: got the guide, silent**
- 1st (day 3): `hey [name]. any of it useful?`
- 2nd (day 7): `hey [name]. happy to walk through the setup for your case if useful: [booking link]`
- 3rd (day 14): `hey [name]. no worries if the timing isn't right. the guide is there whenever useful.`
- Stop.

**Banned:** "did you get a chance to check it? curious which part resonated" (his earlier day-3 line, retired as slop), "just checking in", "circling back", "bumping this", "thoughts?", "does that resonate?".

## Replies to replies (answer once, then wait; the founder writes)

| They said | What the reply does |
|---|---|
| "sure" / "please share" / "yes" | Deliver the guide (rotated line above). Sequence B starts. |
| A detailed question about their case | Answer it plainly, then one next step: the booking link. If it needs the founder's judgment (local rules, pricing, a partnership), flag "founder handles". |
| "What are you offering?" | Your message was vague. One plain line on the outcome, then the guide. Log the line that confused them. |
| Asked for a call | `sure. [booking link]` -> founder's calendar. |
| "Not relevant anymore" / changed jobs | `thanks for telling me. good luck with what's next.` -> NURTURE. |
| "No" | `all good, thanks for the reply.` -> never message again. |
