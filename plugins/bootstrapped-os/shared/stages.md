# The `/start-from-zero` stages

The only stage list. Other files name the stage ("the pricing stage"), never its number.

| # | Stage | Role (agent) | Skill | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Idea | `head-of-research` + `idea-critic` | idea-finder | Claude, a visible Chrome |
| 2 | Positioning | `head-of-positioning` | positioning | Claude, Chrome |
| 3 | Brand | `brand-designer` | brand-brief | Claude |
| 4 | Pricing | `cfo` | pricing-audit | Claude, Chrome |
| 5 | Landing page | `head-of-design` | landing-page | the software module's launch starter |
| 6 | Deploy | `head-of-infrastructure` | deploy | GitHub, Vercel, Supabase |
| 7 | Product, payments and login | Head of Engineering + `cfo` | head-of-engineering, payments-agentaos | the software module, an AgentaOS test account |
| 8 | Launch video | `creative-director` | launch-video | the video module |
| 9 | Launch and first customers | `head-of-growth`, `head-of-sales`, `head-of-content` | launch, sales-outreach, lead-magnets | a domain, your accounts |

One stage at a time. Each one ends with its result and "go to <next stage>?". If a stage needs a module you haven't installed, say which one and skip it.
