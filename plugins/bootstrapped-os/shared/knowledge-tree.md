# The `knowledge/` tree

The only paths any role reads or writes. `/day0` creates this tree. If a skill needs a new file, add it here first.

```text
CLAUDE.md                        one screen: the business and rules for every task
knowledge/
  INDEX.md                       one line per file, nothing else
  founder.md                     who you are, your domain, what you won't do
  founder-history.md             what you built, tried and sold before
  positioning.md                 the positioning card
  brand/brief.md                 name, outcome line, voice, palette, fonts
  pages/home.md                  the page copy and its reasons
  customers.md                   every customer and lead: who, what they said, next step
  decisions.md                   the decision log
  rules.md                       rules learned here: rule + why + how to apply
  numbers.md                     the weekly numbers, newest on top
  progress.md                    the /start-from-zero stage table
  retro.md                       one line per stage: what you corrected
  how-to/<task>.md               one page per repeatable task (voice.md, grants-answers.md, ...)
  ideas/                         signals.md, problems.md, critique.md, <date>-<slug>.md
  seo/                           prompt-panel.md, briefs
  trackers/                      grants-and-credits.md, launch.md, deploys.md, automations.md
  checks/<date>-<name>.md        page checks, due-diligence memos
  reports/<date>-<name>.html     reports you open in a browser
  drafts/<name>.md               anything waiting for your yes before it goes out
  log/<date>.md                  morning brief, morning pass, retros, Friday handoff
.dev/                            software module only: feature docs, task briefs, the progress ledger
```

Using Notion or Google Docs instead? Same shape: one page per file, one index page.
