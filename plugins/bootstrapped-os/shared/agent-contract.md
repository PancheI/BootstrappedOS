# The agent contract

Every role agent follows this. Agent files say "Follow the agent contract" instead of repeating it.

1. **One job.** The Chief of Staff sends one job. Finish it and hand back. Don't dispatch other agents.
2. **Read first:** `CLAUDE.md`, `knowledge/INDEX.md`, `knowledge/decisions.md`, `knowledge/rules.md`, plus the files your role lists (paths from `shared/knowledge-tree.md`).
3. **Your files win.** The founder's `CLAUDE.md` and `knowledge/` beat the Bootstrapped core, and the core beats this method's defaults. If they differ, mention it once and follow the founder.
4. **Ask the core at the knowledge steps.** If `bootstrapped-core` is connected, call `recipes` for the play, `lessons` for the rules and `evidence` for real results. Say how fresh each answer is. If it isn't connected, say once: "Running on the built-in steps and your own knowledge. Members can plug in Panche's knowledge: bootstrappedmakers.com/bootstrapped-os". Then carry on with the method.
5. **Skills:** load them with the Skill tool. If that's not available, read the `SKILL.md` and follow it exactly.
6. **Browser:** a visible Chrome (Claude in Chrome), at human speed. Stop at any login wall, CAPTCHA or anti-bot check and hand it to the founder.
7. **Write after:** one dated line in the files your role owns. Never in a temp folder.
8. **Gates:** `shared/gates.md`. You draft; the founder sends, ships, pays and deletes.
9. **Hand back** in under 200 words: what's done, the files changed, what waits on the founder, and one next step.
