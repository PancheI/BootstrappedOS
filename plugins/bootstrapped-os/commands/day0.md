Use the `chief-of-staff` skill for $ARGUMENTS.

**Read first:** `CLAUDE.md` if one exists, `knowledge/` if it exists.

1. **Run the tools check** (the skill's `references/tools-check.md`) and show me the table. Then go one step at a time and wait for my answer after each question.
2. **Ask me 5 questions:**
   - What kind of business? (software / agency or services / online shop / creator, coach or course / other)
   - Which tools do I already use for email, payments, analytics, notes and social?
   - My goal this month? (first customers / more revenue / save time / launch something / grants and credits)
   - How do I ship things? (code / no-code site builder / I don't)
   - Revenue today? ($0 / under $1k a month / more)
3. **Write my CLAUDE.md** in this project from the skill's `references/claude-md-template.md`, filled with my answers. One screen of rule lines; the why and how go in `knowledge/rules.md`. Show it before saving. If one exists, add a section instead of replacing it.
4. **Create the shared knowledge base:** the `knowledge/` tree in `shared/knowledge-tree.md`, using the skill's `references/knowledge-templates.md` (section 3). Write my first decisions in `knowledge/decisions.md`, dated, each as a rule, why, and how to apply it:
   - "Agents draft, I send."
   - "I approve by number: 'go 1 3' does those, locally. 'yes' or 'ok' does nothing. Only 'ship' or 'deploy' means production."

   Write one line per file in `knowledge/INDEX.md`.
5. **Recommend modules and connectors:** software -> `bootstrapped-os-software`; a website I want to sell more from -> `bootstrapped-os-growth`; videos -> `bootstrapped-os-video`. Give the exact install lines. Connectors only for the tools I named (`references/connectors.md`).
6. **Match the team to my stage.**
   - **$0 or under $1k a month:** lead with Sales, Launch, Content and Grants. Say plainly that the support inbox, failed-payment and win-back emails, /money-review and /run-ops can wait until customers arrive, and leave them out of my rhythm for now.
   - **More:** the whole team.
7. **Three first wins, for my business:**
   - At $0 or under $1k: /first-customers (Day 0: my buyer, the problem in their words, my price), /grants (credits and perks I qualify for, checked today), and /launch-week or /post-from-a-win.
   - Otherwise: /grants, /write-emails (my welcome email) or /weekly-review (my 5 numbers), and one more that fits my goal: /launch-week, /triage-inbox, /post-from-a-win, or (software) /feature on a tiny change.

**Hand back:** what's set up, where it lives, and my rhythm. At $0: /first-customers daily, /weekly-review on Mondays, /grants monthly. Later: /morning daily, /weekly-review on Mondays, /grants and /money-review monthly. Gates: `shared/gates.md`.
