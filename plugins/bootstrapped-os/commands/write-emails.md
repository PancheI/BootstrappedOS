Use the `customer-emails` skill and the `head-of-support` agent for $ARGUMENTS.

**Read first:** my site, `knowledge/how-to/voice.md`, `knowledge/decisions.md` (offers, refund policy).

1. Write each email as a draft in this shape: subject = the fact, preview line = the reassurance, 2-3 short lines (under 80 words, under 60 is better), one first-person button, my first name. No prices except in the renewal notice.
2. For each email give the trigger, when it stops, and the rule that makes it go only once per person.
3. Rules for the series: one a day at most, none on sign-up day, each kind once, each one skips itself when its action is done, and the series ends with "Should I stop writing to you?".
4. Reviewing existing emails? For each: the word count, what to cut, and the line to replace (not add).
5. Save the drafts to `knowledge/drafts/<name>.md`.

**Hand back:** the drafts, with what I need to confirm marked. Real facts only. Gates: `shared/gates.md`.
