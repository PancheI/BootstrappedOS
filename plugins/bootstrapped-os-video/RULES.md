# Video module: shared rules

Every skill in this module follows these rules. Skills link here instead of repeating them.

## 1. Advanced, optional
Start with `launch-video`. It needs only a phone, a screen recorder and any editor. The other skills are the advanced route: films built in code (Remotion) with generated voice and music (ElevenLabs). Use them when the simple film works and you want studio-grade motion.

## 2. Length
- **A launch video is about 30 seconds.** "Always make sure the videos are around 30 seconds, that's the max launch video."
- **60 seconds only for a walkthrough** of a website or product, when the founder asks for one.
- A reel is 15 to 30 seconds.

## 3. Originality first, and references you have the right to use
- **The goal is an original film.** References teach timing and pacing. They are never the thing you copy.
- **Use references you have the right to study.** Before you download a film, check the platform's terms. When in doubt, ask the creator.
- **Never work around logins, paywalls, captchas or download blocks.** If a site doesn't let you download, pick another reference.
- **Copy craft, never assets.** Never reuse a reference's music, voice, logo, footage, screenshots or brand names. Only its measured timing and motion.
- **No single reference above 25 % of the runtime.** Combine several, with a different influence per beat.

## 4. Measure motion, don't invent it (the author's rule)
Every move on screen (camera path, easing, stagger, slam, roll, press, transition) is measured from a real reference, frame by frame, and played back from the measured track. If no reference has the move you need, find one (`video-reference-scout`) before building. Record the source of every move: beat, move, reference timestamp, track file.

This is the author's taste: his films are "measured, not invented". It's a strong default for the advanced route, not a law. Hand-made motion is fine in a simple `launch-video` cut.

## 5. Your brand, from your files
Palette, fonts, logo and name come from `knowledge/brand/brief.md` (the brand stage). Never a reference's palette, and never a redrawn logo: use the real logo file. Product screens are real captures.

## 6. Paid generation needs a yes
Voice and music cost credits. Lock the script first, show the cost (characters or seconds), and get the founder's yes before any paid call. Gates: `plugins/bootstrapped-os/shared/gates.md` (core).

## 7. Nothing goes out without the founder
The agent makes the files and shows them. Posting and publishing wait for the founder.
