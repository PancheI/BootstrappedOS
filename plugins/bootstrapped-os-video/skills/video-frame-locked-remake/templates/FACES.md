# Avatar portraits (not included)

The `<Avatar>` component in `core.tsx` can show photo portraits from `public/remake/faces/f00.jpg … fNN.jpg`.
No portraits ship with this pack. To use them, add your own AI-generated portraits of people who do not exist
(generated by you, or licensed for commercial use), then set `FACES` (count) and `FACE_GENDER`
(one letter per face: m / w) in `core.tsx`. With `FACES = 0`, avatars fall back to initials.
