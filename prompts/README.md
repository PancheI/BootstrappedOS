# Prompts that work (copy-paste)

Commands marked (software module) or (growth module) need that module installed.

**Daily**
- "/morning"
- "What's the one thing that would move revenue this week? Use my numbers, not guesses."
- "/run-ops", then "go 1 3" (do proposals 1 and 3; nothing else runs)

**Sales**
- "Find me 3 to 5 posts on X, LinkedIn or Reddit from the last week that ask a question my product answers. Link each one. Don't write replies."
- "/first-customers"

**Engineering** (software module)
- "/feature <one-line change>"
- "Read .dev/progress.md and tell me where we are and what's next."
- "/review-branch", then "/ship"
- "Write a handoff note before we stop."

**Growth**
- "/critique-page https://yoursite.com" (growth module)
- "Give me 3 options for this hero: bold, middle and safe. One headline, one line, one button, the price next to it."

**Analytics**
- "/weekly-review"
- "/check-funnel" (growth module)
- "Pick the 3 most engaged and 2 fastest-leaving replays this week and tell me where they hesitated."

**Content**
- "/post-from-a-win <what happened, with the real number>"
- "Turn this week's best result into a 30-second video beat sheet." (video module)
